# Evidence ledger — India-first construction intelligence

Status: Best effort. Gaps remain on exhaustive local-startup coverage and independently verified traction/pricing.

1. Snaptrude (primary, read): brief-to-BIM; Site Analysis, Programming, Massing, BIM, Presentation; prompt/RFP + site program generation; building regulations/zoning/site constraints research; live-linked tables; area/solar analysis; AI renders/collaboration. https://www.snaptrude.com/

2. TestFit Site Solver (primary, read): parcel-to-pro-forma; zoning; building configurators; side-by-side schemes; code/regulations; cut/fill; quantity takeoff; site intelligence; report builder; pro forma. Targets developers, architects, contractors, urban planners, civil engineers. https://www.testfit.io/product/site-solver

3. Archistar (primary, read): 3D generative design; site summaries; property/market listings; rules/risks/reports/feasibility; Nearmap aerial imagery and Cordell development listings. Its paid coverage page is Australia-focused, leaving a possible India localization gap. https://www.archistar.ai/pricing/

4. BIS NBC 2016 (primary government, read): model code for PWDs, local bodies and private agencies; covers administration, development control, fire/life safety, materials, structural design, sustainability and facility management; 12 parts/33 chapters; integrated/single-window and computerized approval provisions. https://www.bis.gov.in/standards/national-building-code/?lang=en

5. Bhuvan thematic services (primary ISRO/NRSC, read): users can select/query thematic datasets and integrate them as OGC web services; interface exposes land-use/land-cover, land degradation and urban land-use themes. https://bhuvan-app1.nrsc.gov.in/thematic/thematic/index.php

6. Official data stack (search/read mix): NDMA/NRSC flood hazard atlases, landslide hazard maps, GSI geological data; IMD climate services/data portal; NRSC flood atlas based on 1998–2022 satellite study. https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/hydrological_fhz.php https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/geological_lshz.php https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/geological_gsi.php https://mausam.imd.gov.in/responsive/climate_services.php https://ndem.nrsc.gov.in/documents/downloads/Flood%20Affected%20Area%20%20Atlas%20of%20India%20-Satellite%20based%20study.pdf

7. Infra.Market (primary, read): tech-enabled building-materials platform with pan-India manufacturing and project-lifecycle portfolio; catalogue includes concrete, aggregates, steel, chemicals, walling, plumbing, plywood, roofing, etc. https://infra.market/ https://infra.market/catalogue

8. Moglix (primary, read): infrastructure procurement for Indian NIP sectors; page states 6+ raw-material categories, 40+ consumable categories, 45,000+ suppliers, approved supplier lists/brands, cost optimization across project lifecycle. Cement page names price volatility, logistics/vendor management and quality compliance as pains; targets MSMEs, PSUs, contractors, large real-estate firms. https://business.moglix.com/our-solutions/infrastructure https://business.moglix.com/industry/cement

9. Inncircles (primary, read): planning, contract management and cost management with live visibility; owners, contractors, PMCs; buildings, roads, bridges, industrial and other infrastructure. https://beta.inncircles.com/

10. BuildEstimate AI (primary, read; metrics self-reported): blueprint-to-BOQ; claims 5,000+ professionals, ₹1,200 Cr+ estimates, IS/CPWD compliance; local rates, CPWD SOR, material list, client report and structural AI; disclaims licensed engineering/approval service and requires engineer/site verification for high accuracy. https://www.buildestimateai.com/

11. BuildNet (primary, read; claims self-reported): AI design/estimation/reports, marketplace, assistant accepting blueprints/BOQ/docs/images/audio and construction questions; free/₹1,000 monthly/custom plans. https://buildnet.app/

12. C&D reuse (primary government, read): PIB/MoHUA release says India generates 150–500 Mt C&D waste annually; NCR 6,303 TPD and ~78% processed per day; government emphasizes segregation, collection, reuse, recycling and utilization. CPCB portal provides EPR, authorized recycler linkage and utilization tracking. 2016 rules require project waste-management plan assessing quantities. https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2007086 https://cdwm.cpcb.gov.in/ https://thc.nic.in/Central%20Governmental%20Rules/Construction%20and%20Demolition%20Waste%20Management%20Rules,%202016.pdf

13. Materialyz (primary page read; India location only secondary): industrial raw-material marketplace with surplus material management, sustainable resource management and buyer/seller registration. https://materialyz.com/pages/about-us1 ; profile https://platform.tracxn.com/a/d/company/5ff5e1a80e50e51eca050f6b/materialyz

14. SurplusMart should not be treated as active competitor: domain currently parks at GoDaddy. https://surplusmart.in/

15. Inference: generic AI BOQ/render/Q&A is increasingly commoditized (BuildEstimate AI + BuildNet + Snaptrude); defensibility should come from India-specific data fusion, evidence/auditability, workflows and outcomes, not the LLM alone.
